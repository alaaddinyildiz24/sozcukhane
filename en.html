Privacy Policy — Sözcükhane

Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. Who we are

Sözcükhane (the "Game") is a Turkish word game published on Google Play by bamy24.

Contact: 06.bamy24@gmail.com

2. What data the Game collects

The Game collects no personal data and sends no data to us or to anyone else.

  • There are no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • The Game does not use an internet connection; the app has no internet permission.
  • The Game shows no ads.
  • The Game has no analytics (statistics) or crash reporting service.

3. What is stored on your device

Your game progress, coins, found words (Word Book), achievements and settings are stored only on your device. They never reach us.

Uninstalling the Game deletes this data from the device.

4. Android backup

If Android backup is turned on, Android may back up the file that holds your progress and settings to your own Google Account and restore it when you reinstall the Game. The backup is kept in a private area of your Google Account; we cannot access it.

You can turn backup off in your device Settings > System > Backup (menu names vary by device). The backup in your Google Account is kept according to Android's and Google's rules.

5. Third-party services

This version has no third-party service that collects or receives data. If a service such as ads or crash reporting is added later, this policy will be updated together with that version of the Game.

6. Age limit

The Game is not directed to people under 16.

7. Changes

If we update this policy we will publish the new text and change the "Last updated" date. The current text can also be read in the Game under Settings > Privacy policy.
