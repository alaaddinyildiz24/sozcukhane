English

Data we collect. Firefly Jars does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name or email. Your game progress (level, daily streak, settings) is stored only on your device and is not sent to us.

Ads. The game uses Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect data such as the advertising ID, device information, approximate location (from IP) and ad interactions to serve and measure ads and prevent abuse. Details: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Consent and your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you are asked for ad consent on first launch. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → "Ad privacy choices". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Children. The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Deleting data. Game data lives on your device; uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of it.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new date is shown on this page.
